User Guide

Manti.S Quickstart Guide

The MANTI-S estimates NDVI from red and near-infrared sensor readings, using the characterised transmission of its top diffuser.

Taking measurements

Using the device

  1. Hold the Manti.S over the area to be measured.
  2. Press and release the trigger briefly to take a measurement.
  3. For best results, hold the device parallel to the ground when taking measurements.
  4. Use the device during daylight hours.
  5. Ensure that the white diffuser on the top of the device is clean of dirt and debris.

Device setup

Wi-Fi setup and firmware updates

Wi-Fi stays off during normal measurements. Open setup only when you need to connect the device to the internet or install an update.

  1. Hold the trigger for 5 seconds until the device shows SETUP / UPDATE, then release it.
  2. On your phone, join the Manti-S Wi-Fi network shown on the device. Enter the eight-character password shown on the next line of its screen. The password changes each time setup opens.
  3. If your phone warns that this network has no internet, stay connected. Open http://192.168.4.1/ in a browser if the setup page does not appear automatically.
  4. Select an internet Wi-Fi network in the portal, enter its password, and press Connect to Wi-Fi. If your network is missing, press Rescan networks to refresh the list. The Manti-S network remains available while the device joins that network.
  5. Press Check for update. If a release is available, connect USB power and press Install update. Checking alone never installs firmware.

If your phone switches away from the Manti-S network, reconnect to it to continue using the portal. When finished, press the device trigger once or choose Finish setup. The portal closes and Wi-Fi turns off. A successful firmware installation restarts the device.

Updates become available only after a public firmware release is published. The device never installs a new release automatically.

Measurement Conditions

Things that affect your values

Differing sensor light conditions

The Manti.S uses a matched pair of sensors to calculate NDVI. This means the white diffuser on the top of the device must be in the same light conditions as the area being measured, otherwise values will be skewed.

Wet ground

Ratios of IR and red light from wet soil can inflate measurements.

Low light

When the device indicates "low light" it is sensing weak signals from the bottom sensor and measurements may be partially less accurate.
